# Invoza

**Free invoicing tools for freelancers, contractors, and small businesses.**

Create professional invoices, estimates, and payment receipts directly in your browser — no signup required.

🌐 **Live:** https://getinvoza.com

---

## What is Invoza?

Invoza is a free browser-based invoicing toolkit designed to make billing simple.

You can:

- Create professional invoices
- Create project estimates
- Convert approved estimates into invoices
- Create payment receipts
- Download clean PDF documents
- Save reusable business and client details locally
- Reuse frequently billed services
- Duplicate previous invoices
- Keep recent invoice history in your browser
- Export and import your saved Invoza data

No account is required.

---

## Business Document Workflow

Invoza supports a simple billing lifecycle:

**Estimate → Invoice → Payment → Receipt**

### Estimate

Create a professional estimate before work begins.

Add:

- Project details
- Scope of work
- Exclusions
- Line items
- Taxes and discounts
- Deposit requirements
- Estimate validity

Once approved, the estimate can be transferred into the invoice generator without retyping the job.

👉 https://getinvoza.com/estimate-generator

### Invoice

Create a professional invoice with:

- Business and client details
- Invoice numbers
- Issue and due dates
- Line items
- Taxes
- Discounts
- Payment terms
- Notes
- Multiple currencies
- Live preview
- Professional PDF export

👉 https://getinvoza.com/invoice

### Receipt

After payment is received, create a professional payment receipt containing:

- Receipt number
- Payment date
- Customer details
- Invoice reference
- Payment method
- Transaction reference
- Itemized amounts

👉 https://getinvoza.com/receipt-generator

## Built for Repeat Invoicing

Invoza can optionally save reusable information locally in your browser.

### Saved Business Profile

Reuse your:

- Business name
- Contact details
- Address
- Logo
- Tax or business ID
- Default currency
- Payment terms

### Saved Clients

Save frequently billed clients and reuse their details on future documents.

### Saved Services

Save common services and rates so repeat invoices take less time to create.

### Recent Invoice History

Keep recent invoices locally and:

- Open previous invoices
- Duplicate an invoice
- Edit a new copy
- Remove old records

Invoza currently keeps a lightweight local history of recent invoices.

---

## Local Backup

Your reusable Invoza data can be exported as a JSON backup.

You can later import that backup to restore or move:

- Business details
- Saved clients
- Saved services
- Recent invoice history

This happens without requiring an Invoza account.

---

## Privacy

Document creation and PDF generation happen directly in your browser.

Optional reusable information such as business details, clients, services, drafts, and recent invoice data can be stored in your browser's local storage.

That means:

- No account is required
- Saved data stays on that browser/device
- Browser-local storage is not cloud storage
- Clearing browser storage can remove locally saved information
- You can export a backup if you want to preserve or move your saved data
- Invoza does not claim browser local storage is encrypted

Do not store information in Invoza that you would not normally keep on your own device.

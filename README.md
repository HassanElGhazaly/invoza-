# Invoza

> Simple invoices. Done in seconds.

Invoza is a free online invoice generator that helps freelancers, contractors, and small businesses create professional invoices quickly without signing up.

**Official Website:** [https://getinvoza.com](https://getinvoza.com)

---

## What is Invoza?

Invoza is a fast, web-based invoice creation tool designed to remove friction from billing clients. It runs directly inside modern web browsers, allowing users to enter billing details, format itemized line items, calculate taxes and discounts automatically, and download a publication-grade vector PDF invoice in seconds.

Invoza requires no account registration, login credentials, or software installation.

---

## Who is it for?

Invoza is built for professionals who need clean, accurate invoices without the complexity or recurring cost of heavyweight accounting software:

- **Freelancers**: Designers, developers, copywriters, and consultants billing clients on an hourly, milestone, or flat-fee basis.
- **Independent Contractors & Trades**: Electricians, plumbers, builders, technicians, and mechanics providing itemized work estimates and invoices.
- **Small Business Owners**: Boutique agencies, studios, consultancies, and shops needing reliable ad-hoc invoicing with custom logos and tax calculations.
- **Sole Proprietors & Service Providers**: Photographers, tutors, event planners, and virtual assistants seeking a straightforward billing tool.

---

## Key Features

- **100% Free**: Full access to all invoicing features with no fees, trial periods, or hidden charges.
- **No Signup Required**: Create and download invoices immediately with zero registration.
- **6 Professional Invoice Templates**: Thoughtfully styled templates (Modern, Minimal, Professional, Classic, Elegant, and Bold) engineered for clarity and typography.
- **Real-Time Live Preview**: Visual confirmation of layout, spacing, and styling changes as you type.
- **Automatic Calculations**: Instant calculation of subtotal, flat or percentage-based discounts, regional taxes (Sales Tax, VAT, GST), and final balance due.
- **Custom Business Branding**: Upload and position high-resolution company logos directly on your invoice.
- **Itemized Line Items**: Detailed rows for products and services with quantity counters, unit rates, and automatic line totals.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Support for major global currencies including USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), CAD ($), AUD ($), JPY (¥), and more.
- **Client-Side PDF Generation**: Fast, vector-quality PDF rendering executed directly inside the user's browser with no watermarks.

## How It Works

Creating an invoice with Invoza involves three simple steps:

1. **Enter Invoice Details**: Fill in your business name, contact info, client information, invoice number, issue date, and payment due date.
2. **Add Line Items & Adjust Rates**: Add item descriptions, quantities, unit prices, any applicable discounts, and local tax rates (VAT/GST/Sales Tax).
3. **Download PDF**: Review the live preview, choose your preferred design template, and click "Download PDF" to receive an unwatermarked, print-ready document.

---

## Privacy & Client-Side Processing

Invoza is engineered with client-side privacy as a core design principle:

- **Browser-Based Execution**: All invoice compilation and PDF generation logic runs entirely in client memory inside your browser using JavaScript.
- **No Database Storage or Transmission**: Invoza does not store, transmit, or archive your business details, client contacts, line items, or financial figures on remote servers.
- **No Local Storage or Disk Persistence**: Invoice data is held strictly in transient in-memory state during your active browser session. It is never written to LocalStorage, SessionStorage, or local disk, and is cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.

## Project Purpose & Positioning

Invoza was created to solve a widespread problem: small businesses and freelancers frequently need to issue clean, professional invoices without being forced to sign up for expensive monthly accounting suites or surrender private financial records to third-party databases.

### Important Positioning

Invoza is a web-based invoice creation tool, not an accounting firm, bank, payment processor, or financial institution. Invoza does not manage bank accounts, collect client payments, transfer funds, or submit tax returns.
